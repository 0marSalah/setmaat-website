Set + Maat

In ancient Egypt, Maat stood for truth, fairness and order, drawn as a single feather. SetMaat means “setting Maat in place”. Today the scale tips toward the platforms that hold our data. We build tools that set it right.

Why we exist

Every team building small, local AI ends up rebuilding the same thing: somewhere safe to keep a person's context, and a way to decide who can read it. We build that backpack once, in the open, so people can see what's in it and how it's used, choose who and what gets access, and carry it between tools instead of being locked into one.

  • You hold the keys
  • Open standards
  • Open source, always
  • No lock-in
  • Non-profit

What we do

Three programs, chosen and funded by members. Everything we make is released openly, for anyone to run, adapt, fork and build on.

01

The Agentic Digital Backpack Platform

The storage servers, wallets and libraries behind the backpack. They give a small AI project encrypted storage and fine-grained authorization without building them from scratch. Fully open source, self-hostable, free to use, study and build on.

02

Interop through open standards

We take part in the standards these tools rest on, like Portable Web Spaces at the W3C Credentials Community Group, so projects from different teams can read and write the same data instead of each inventing a format.

03

Learning circles

Study groups, online and in person, where members and teams adopting these tools work through storage, keys and authorization together, from first key pair to production.

The storage

PWS Spaces

Portable Web Spaces: where the contents of the backpack are kept.

PWS is storage built on the Portable Web Spaces specification from the W3C Credentials Community Group. Each space belongs to its owner, and every app, model or agent only gets the access it was granted.

  • Organise data into Spaces and Collections, each with its own permissions
  • Encrypt collections so the server storing them can't read them
  • Export a space and import it elsewhere, or sync it across servers you choose

The keys

Freewallet

A free, open-source wallet for the keys and permissions that open the backpack.

Freewallet runs in your browser and keeps your keys on your side. There is no login server holding your account: you unlock it with a passkey or a passphrase, and from there you hand out exactly the access each app or AI agent needs.

  • Hold your DIDs, verifiable credentials and keys yourself, not on a platform
  • Give an app or AI agent scoped, revocable access instead of handing over everything
  • Syncs your wallet to PWS Spaces you choose

Agentic Digital Backpack Platform

One place for the data your agents work from, owned by you.

The backpack is what you get when storage you control meets keys you hold. It is made of two open-source tools that work today, and it runs wherever you choose to keep it.

What goes in

Assistant context and memory, notes and markdown files, sensitive records, a backup of the family photo archive.

Who can reach in

Each app, model or agent gets only the access you grant it, scoped to what it needs, and you can take it back at any time.

Take it anywhere

Export it, import it elsewhere, sync it across servers you choose, or host it yourself at home or on-prem.
