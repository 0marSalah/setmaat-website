Four ways the hero could speak

Same message — own your identity, own your data — in four different voices. The first is today's site for reference; the three below trade some of its loudness for warmth, clarity, or a more measured kind of boldness. Pick a direction and I'll build it live in the React app.

A · Current

Loud, monumental, activist

CommunicatesConviction and defiance. Reads like a protest poster: principled, rooted in place, a movement more than a product.
Trade-offAll-caps display at this density can feel aggressive and leans industrial — less welcoming to non-technical visitors.

Inviting, people-first, trustworthy
